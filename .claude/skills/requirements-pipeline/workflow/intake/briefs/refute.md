# Refute: try to break a ticket's combined backlog

You did not write this backlog, the intake record or the investigation. Try to refute every row, every split, every
statement and every question. Holding is a finding you reach, never the default. You write no file.

## Read first

- The same ticket file, attachments, rulings and code the reader read.
- `investigation.md`, `intake.json` and `backlog.json` beside them.
- `.claude/skills/requirements-pipeline/references/SHAPE.md` ("Ticket backlogs"), and
  `workflow/distil/briefs/combine-review.md` for the reasons a second row may stand.

## What to check

- **Each row:** does each criterion follow from a statement; is it observable; is anything the ticket asks for
  missing (a verdict of `missed`); is the row a full-stack slice.
- **Each split:** when more than one row is still to build, does the reason it cannot join another row really hold,
  or should it join one.
- **Each statement:** is the cover real; is a not-built reason sound; are the words the ticket's own.
- **Each question:** does the ticket, a rulings ledger or a ruling source already settle it; does it really hold the
  launch for one of the five causes.

## What to answer

One verdict for each id your prompt lists, no more and no fewer:

- a row: `holds`, `wrong` or `missed`;
- a split: `holds` or `wrong`;
- a statement: `holds`, `wrong` or `missed`;
- a question: `stands`, `settled` (the ticket or a ruling settles it) or `drop`.

Every verdict that is not `holds` or `stands` carries what is wrong and what the reader should do, in plain words.
