## Summary
Adds an animals end-to-end test that seeds nine consignor records behind one token and proves 8 a page, the search term kept and the chosen address named on page 2. It moves the existing picker specs from "whole list" and "nothing between search and results" assertions to a paged count and the "Selected address" line.

## Positive Observations
- The new animals paging test controls its own data, so its counts are exact however much else is in the shared address book.
- `expectChosenAddressNamedAboveResults` checks both the wording and where it sits on the page.

## Test Coverage
- E2E: animals paging is covered. Plants has no paging end-to-end test, although the ticket says "in either journey".
- The animals test at line 52 kept its old title, which says "lists every match on one page". The workspace coverage links cite a new title that no test has, so four links point at nothing.

## Risk Assessment
**Overall Risk:** Medium
**Rationale:** The retitle the coverage links rely on is missing, a page-1 absence check can no longer fail, and the PR adds pagination locators that duplicate ones `BasePage` already has.
