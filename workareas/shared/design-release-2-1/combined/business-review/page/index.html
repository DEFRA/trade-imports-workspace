DR2.1 business review

Every change a user would notice in Design Release 2.1 has a card.

For each card, tell us: is this the right change, from a business point of view?

The review covers 3 services:

  • live animals
  • high-risk plants
  • the Import Notification Service front door (the home page and address book)

"Before" shows the service on the main branch today.

"After" shows the combined branch. It holds every finished change.

The type question and templates (EUDPA-690) are in the combined branch too. Their cards come last.

Nine of their rows are still to be built, so more cards will be added later.

The Design Release 2.1 prototype is the authority on look and feel only. Look and feel means:

  • wording
  • layout
  • components
  • question order

The prototype does not decide how the services work behind the page.

Where a card shows a prototype screen, it is there to compare the look.

How to answer

  1. Read the open questions first.
  2. Then read the changes, in journey order.
  3. Compare "Before" and "After". You can scroll inside a picture, or open it at "Full size".
  4. Each card has a section called "What the spec says". It shows what the written specification used to say, and what it says now. Open a change to read both.
  5. Choose "Right", "Wrong" or "Not sure". The page saves your choice straight away.
  6. If you can say why, or what should change, add a comment. Then select "Save comment".
  7. Everyone's answers show under each card.
