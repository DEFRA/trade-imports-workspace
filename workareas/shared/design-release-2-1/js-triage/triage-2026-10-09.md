## What the libraries offer (as checked)

- **GOV.UK Frontend** has no modal, copy button, date picker, type-ahead or live filter. Its patterns have no "add another", filter or search pattern.
- **HMRC** has "Add to a list", which is server-side, and a timeout pattern. It has no modal, copy or search pattern.
- **MoJ Frontend 11.1.0** is installed in the animals frontend, not in the INS frontend. It has a date picker, filter, sortable table, multi-select, button menu, alert, add another, search and sub navigation.
  - The date picker is Official. MoJ warns it is slow for keyboard and screen reader users, and says not to use it for dates users can look up.
  - The Modal dialog (Dec 2025) and Copy button (Jan 2026) are Experimental and not in 11.1.0. The modal documents no fallback without JavaScript. The copy button is hidden without JavaScript.
- **accessible-autocomplete** supports contains-matching, bold matches and `showAllValues:false`. It does not support a "Search" button beside the box, a custom "Selected X" announcement, or several selections in one box.

## Questions for design

1. **Search buttons:** the country, port and transit boxes show results as you type, so a magnifying-glass "Search" button does nothing. Can we drop it?
2. **Region code:** can the country prefix (for example "FR-") appear after saving, rather than changing as the country is picked?
3. **Commodity search:** can results show after pressing Search, as today, rather than as the trader types?
4. **Transit countries:** can we keep the "Add country" and "Remove" buttons that reload the page, rather than adding a country the moment it is picked?
5. **Live filtering:** can the transporter list, the address book list and the roles and addresses pickers filter on Search or Enter, rather than as the trader types? This one needs the product owner too, because of claim 22 and c-094.
6. **Place of origin picker:** can the trader choose a country and press Search, rather than the rows filtering as they type?
7. **CPH number:** can the pages accept what is typed or pasted and check it on save? Would one box that accepts "12/345/6789" work instead of three?
8. **Copy buttons:** are they needed next to the reference and customs document code, which are shown as selectable text anyway?
9. **Amend and cancel:** can confirmations stay as separate pages, rather than pop-ups? GOV.UK has no pop-up component.
10. **Address search (once unblocked):** can it use the GOV.UK address pattern, with the fields always visible?
11. **Templates sort:** "Sort by" does nothing in the prototype. Should we remove it, or make it sort with a button?
12. **Germinal dates:** the dates come from documents the trader has in front of them. Would a plain date field or the GOV.UK date input do, instead of a calendar picker?
