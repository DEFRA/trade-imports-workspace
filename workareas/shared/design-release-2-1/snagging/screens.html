Design Release 2.1 snags: prototype and main side by side

The 38 differences the interaction and content designers listed on 19 October 2026. Each one shows the prototype (commit 56371e2) on the left and the real service built from main on the right, both photographed with Playwright. Screens ending '--nojs' were taken with JavaScript turned off, and '--mobile' at 390px wide. Full reasons are in assessment.md, and the points we want to talk through are in discussion.md.
