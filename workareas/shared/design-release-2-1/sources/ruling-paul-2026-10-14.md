# Ruling: Paul, 14 October 2026

To confirm with Sam. Given while starting the address-book theme ahead of the team's go-ahead, so its rows can build.

14 October, on inc-062 dropping County from the address book's add-details step:

> Keep County.

## Claims

1. The address book's add-details step keeps a 'County (optional)' field between 'Town or city' and 'Postcode or Zip
   code', as the edit page (inc-073) and the view page (inc-064) already do. Replaces inc-062's criterion "The
   add-details step has no County field".
2. Why: the Confluence page "Live Animals & Germinals Data Fields - V4" (`confluence:6497338582`, Address Block) gives
   every address block the same fields, County included, and it ranks above the prototype. Where a Design Release 2.1
   page leaves County out, that is a prototype mistake (EUDPA-646 ruling, 1 October 2026,
   `workareas/shared/catch-up-address-book-with-prototype-21/sources/ruling-paul-2026-10-01.md`). An add form that
   cannot set a field the record, the view and the edit form all carry would also lose data on a round trip.
