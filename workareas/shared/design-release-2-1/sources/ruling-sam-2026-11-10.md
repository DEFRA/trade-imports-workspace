# Ruling: Sam, 10 November 2026

Decided by Sam while reviewing what in the waiting-on-design theme could be built before the session with the
new interaction designer.

> I don't think germline suits being in the waiting-on-design thing. Germlines will just match in with animals
> as a reference point for everything. If anything, fold this in the Germinals date calendar into one of the
> rows on Germinals, but that doesn't suit being separate. Commodities search is being handled by a different
> ticket. Again, the call is blocked until it's been implemented. Don't think you need something confirming a
> transit country has been added or removed when there's a row of transit countries added to a table. What
> confirmation do you need other than it was there, now it isn't, or it wasn't there, now it is?

## Decided

1. **Germinal products follow live animals; they are not a design question.** The germinal pages take live
   animals as their reference for everything. The germinal dates' 'Choose date' calendar (inc-192) leaves the
   waiting-on-design theme and is merged into the germinal identification dates row (inc-053): the dates are
   built with the MOJ date picker the other dates use, as the interaction designer ruled on 9 November.
2. **Commodity search is handled by a separate ticket.** The design call on results as you type (inc-014) stays
   blocked until that ticket has been implemented, and does not go to the designer before then. The joined
   search button and the 'cattle' search listing germinal codes belong to that ticket, not to this backlog.
3. **Transit countries need no extra confirmation.** The added-countries table already shows a country
   appearing or going. No visible message is added for an add or remove without JavaScript.
