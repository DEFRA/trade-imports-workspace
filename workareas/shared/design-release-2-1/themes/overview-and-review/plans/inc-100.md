## What the live tree does today

- `flow/task-rows.js:76` — `{ id: 'transitCountries', pages: [transitCountriesPage], conditional: true }`.
  No `applies`. Its status is the roll-up of `transitedCountries`, whose obligation
  (`obligations/sections/transport.js`) is in scope, optional, only for
  `meansOfTransport` RAILWAY or ROAD_VEHICLE, and out of scope otherwise.
- `features/hub/controller.js` drops a `conditional` row whose status is NA
  (`isHiddenRow`), tags FULFILLED "Complete" and every other status "To do".
- So today: Rail/Road saved → row shown "To do" (OPTIONAL) until a country is saved,
  then "Complete"; Air/Sea/none → row absent. **But with no commodity chosen and
  Road saved, the row still shows "To do"** (the animals-fit trace p4-047/048 —
  the FIT helper `answerArrivalDetails` saves `ROAD_VEHICLE` from happy-path.json).
  That is the one behaviour this row changes.

## 0. Decisions

1. **The rule for "transit countries are required" is the means of transport, plus a
   chosen commodity.** The prototype's `requiresTransitCountries` (routes.js:2307) is
   `meansOfTransport ∈ {Rail, Road}` — the same rule the live obligation and inc-029's
   page redirect already apply. AC3 adds "no commodity chosen → not shown", which the
   means alone cannot express. Settled: the row applies when **a commodity is chosen
   AND the means is Rail or Road**. Rejected: (a) the means alone (fails AC3);
   (b) gating the `transitedCountries` obligation itself on the commodity — that
   would purge saved countries whenever the last commodity line is removed, change
   the transit page's own redirect (inc-029, arrival-and-transit's rule) and ripple
   through the reachability analysis; the row's visibility is the hub's question,
   not the obligation's.
2. **Mechanism: an `applies` test on the row**, exactly as the
   `animalIdentification` row does with `identifiesAnAnimal`. The scope condition
   (means) still comes from the parts; `applies` adds the commodity condition.
   Readiness is unaffected: with no commodity the notification is not submittable
   anyway (the commodities row is Not yet started), and NA and OPTIONAL are both
   `rowReady`, so `readyForCheckYourAnswers` and the review's
   `incompleteCardErrors` give the same verdicts as before.
3. **"Poultry" and "pet cats" are the prototype traces' consignments, not the
   deciding answer** (claim that does not hold literally). The traces are
   "poultry-by-rail" (p13-131) and "pet-cats-by-air" (p13-130); the rule they
   witness is the means of transport. The live catalogue has no poultry commodity
   (`services/commodities/stub.js` `COMMODITY_OPTIONS` = Cow, Horse, Cat, Dog,
   Fish). Planned against the rule: AC1 is proved with a chosen commodity (Cow /
   Bos taurus in E2E, Cat in units) travelling by **Rail**; AC2 with **Cat
   (Felis catus) travelling by Air**, and with Cat and no arrival details saved. A
   cat consignment by Road *will* show the row — that is the source rule, not a
   defect.
4. **AC4 already holds** (OPTIONAL → "To do", FULFILLED with ≥1 country →
   "Complete"). No status change; tests pin it. The obligation stays `optional`:
   submission is not newly held up by an empty list (no source asks for that).
5. **Predicate name and home:** a private `hasChosenCommodity` in `task-rows.js`
   (not exported — tests go through `rowStatus` and the hub). Rejected: reusing
   `anyLineApplies` from check-answers (wrong layer, different question).
6. **Germinal half (req-1839) is not touched** — ruling claim 13 splits it to the
   germinal journey row.
7. E2E home: extend `tests/animals/e2e/features/transit-means-scope.spec.ts` (the
   existing Transit-countries-on-the-hub scope spec) rather than a new file.
