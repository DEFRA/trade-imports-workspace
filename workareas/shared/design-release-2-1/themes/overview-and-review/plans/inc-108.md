## 0. Decisions

1. **Criterion 5 (deleted consignor address) describes a state the application cannot reach.** Planned against reality.
   - The ruling it rests on (c-135) assumed a party links back to its address-book record. It does not.
   - REQ-ADDR-002 copies the record onto the notification and keeps no link. SCN-ADDR-002-B pins it: after the record is deleted, the role's row still shows the copy and no error is shown.
   - The e2e test `addresses-copied.spec.ts` "deleting the record in the address book leaves the copy on a draft notification unchanged, with no error…" proves it today.
   - No "Select an address for the consignor" message exists anywhere in the frontend or the tests repo, and the prototype has no deleted-address case either (the c-135 prototype position).
   - What c-135 protects still holds: one summary entry per unfinished card, and no separate role entry. An unanswered role is the case that can happen. Today it gives exactly one entry, "Complete roles and addresses", linking to `#addresses`. `invalidPartyErrors` skips unanswered roles (`check-answers/view-model/invalid-parties.js:13-15`), and the role's cell shows only a visually hidden "Missing".
   - Plan: build no deleted-address state and no new message. The full-list proof for an unanswered role already exists ("Should anchor each entry to the card it names", `check-answers.test.js:2253`, the importer). Section 8 gives it a spec scenario and links it. No duplicate consignor test is added.
   - Rejected: reintroducing a link to the address book. That contradicts REQ-ADDR-002 and SCN-ADDR-013-C, which outrank a ruling that assumed it.
2. **Plants keeps its review's "Edit details" in this row.**
   - Plants' review has no Addresses card. Each party is its own card, with Change and Edit details as card actions (`plants…/check-answers/view-model/index.js:54-77`).
   - Mirroring the "one header link" rule there means deciding plants' card shape. The row's `repos` leave plants out, and the plants party read-back is the next row's (inc-109).
   - Recorded as a departure in section 14 and a follow-up in section 7.
   - Rejected: returning ok:false for plants. Plants' card structure differs, and the rest of the slice stands without it.
3. **Permanent addresses become a "Permanent address" subsection of the Addresses card, through the card `groups` mechanism.**
   - Exemplar: the "Transit countries" group on the Arrival details card (`cards/movement/arrival-details.js:18-49`). The template already draws a card with `groups` as summary rows, then each group's `h4` heading and its own summary list (`check-answers/template.njk:64-98`).
   - The group appears only when at least one animal has a saved permanent address. An animal that still owes one has no entry (REQ-CYA-020 keeps this), so no line needing one means no group.
   - Rejected: keeping the animal rows in the card's main rows. The prototype heads them "Permanent address" (`app/routes.js:5141-5147` at `04a073b`).
4. **A permanent address reads back as name, address lines and country, with no telephone or email.**
   - `rows/party-row.js` keeps sole ownership of how an address reads on the review.
   - Its `partyLines` is split, so the name, address lines and country come from one function. The party rows add the telephone and email after it, and the permanent address entries use that function alone.
   - Party rows keep their telephone and email: SCN-ADDR-002-C and the existing tests pin them, and the party read-back is the next row's (inc-109).
   - Choose the variant by an options object or a separate exported function. Never a positional boolean.
5. **The animal label keeps the species common name as reference data serves it** ("Domestic Cat 1" on the stub).
   - The criterion's "Domestic cat 1" is the prototype's demo data. Programme rule: reference-list content is whatever MDM serves through reference-data, and only how it reads follows the prototype.
   - The label already comes from `permanent-address/saved-addresses.js:22` (`animalLabelOf(line.speciesCommonName, n)`). No change.
6. **The identification table already has no Permanent address column.** That half of criterion 1 is already true: `identification-details-table.js` and `identifier-columns.js` head only the species' scoped identifiers, pinned by `check-answers.test.js:1065`, the FIT test at `check-answers.fit.spec.js:153` and the identification-details spec. No change.
7. **One owner for the CPH number's shape, in a new module in the `cph-number` feature.**
   - The new module owns four pieces, each in one function, and nothing else in the repo restates them:
     - the digit strip of a stored CPH number (drop every non-digit);
     - the nine-digit test;
     - the split of a stored CPH number into its county, parish and holding parts, in that order, by its 2/3/4 digit counts;
     - the slashed read-back ("12/345/6789"), built from that split.
   - `cph-number/validate.js` gives up all four of what it declares today: the digit counts (:16-18), `stripNonDigits` (:26), `NINE_DIGITS` (:28) and the part slicing inside `splitIntoParts` (:30-40). It imports the strip, the nine-digit test and the split from the new module.
   - `validate.js` keeps only what is form-specific: the form field names, mapping the module's county, parish and holding parts onto them (for `fromAnswers` and a whole number typed into the county box), joining the fields back for storage, blankness, and the error copy. Its `requiredExactDigits` calls take their lengths from the module's digit counts.
   - `isWholeNumberInCounty` (:51-55) strips only spaces and slashes before its nine-digit test, not every non-digit. That is a different rule (which separators a whole number typed into one box may carry), so it stays in `validate.js`; its nine-digit test becomes the module's. Rejected: switching it to the module's full strip, which would let "12a3456789" pass as a whole number and change validation.
   - `validate.test.js` staying green, unchanged, proves validation is unchanged.
   - The read-back strips non-digits first, because some stored values already carry slashes (fixtures hold "12/345/6789").
   - A stored value that is not nine digits reads back as stored: `validate.js` already reports it as no longer valid, and slashing a malformed number would misstate it.
   - Both read-backs use it: the review's CPH row (`cards/addresses/addresses.js:62-64`) and the consignment addresses page's CPH inset (`addresses/controller.js:72,78`). deferred.md hands this row the second one.
   - Storage stays nine digits. Rejected: storing slashed (it changes the backend contract and the stored check), and formatting in each caller (two owners).
8. **Row-level "Edit details" leaves the review's party and contact rows.**
   - `partyRow` loses its `editHref` option and the `editAction` helper. `addressesCard` and `contactAddressCard` stop building edit hrefs.
   - `reviewPageTrail` then flows only to the error summary. Drop it from both cards, from `sections/consignment-addresses.js` and `sections/contact-address.js`, and from `buildSections`'s pass-through to them. `buildSections` still receives it only if another section uses it: remove it from `view-model/index.js` if nothing else reads it.
   - The controller's `reviewErrorSummary` keeps linking an invalid role to its edit page with `reviewPageTrail` (`check-answers/controller.js:58-89`). That is criterion 4, unchanged.
   - The consignment addresses page and the contact page keep their own "Edit details" (REQ-ADDR-014): the trader still edits a copy from the page the header Change link opens.
   - The `editDetails` copy key in the check-answers copy (`copy.en.js:6`, `copy.cy.js:7`) becomes unused and is deleted from both locales.
9. **A test title that mispredicts in a touched file is fixed.** `check-answers.test.js:2204` is titled "…carrying a deleted address" but seeds `BROKEN_COPY`. Retitle it to what it proves: a submitted notification with a copy that breaks the rules is not refused.
