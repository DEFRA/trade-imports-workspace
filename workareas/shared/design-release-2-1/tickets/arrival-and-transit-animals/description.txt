h3. Background

Design Release 2.1 makes the means of transport the one answer the arrival details page of a live animals notification cannot be left without, because it decides whether the transit countries page applies. It also gives the transit countries page its own browser title, keeps the page out of the way when a consignment is not arriving by rail or road, and lets a transit country be a territory as well as a country.

Under the 26 October ruling, "Save and return to overview" makes exactly the same checks as "Save and continue". So a missing means of transport is refused on both buttons, and the trader cannot leave arrival details without one. This is a change in behaviour: until now, "Save and return to overview" (and "Save and continue") accepted arrival details with no means of transport chosen and moved on to the next page, and they no longer do. Every other answer on the page can still be left blank and filled in later, as today.

The arrival details wording, the port labels and the transit page's Back link are not part of this ticket: design snagging (EUDPA-720) and reference lists already delivered them.

This ticket covers the *live animals journey only*. The high-risk plants journey has its own ticket, EUDPA-703, and gains no change from this theme. The work is not yet merged.

h3. What changes for the user

h4. A means of transport is required on both save buttons

Saving arrival details with no means of transport chosen, with either "Save and continue" or "Save and return to overview", keeps the trader on the page. The error summary and the means of transport question both say "Select a means of transport to the port of entry", and the browser title starts "Error: ". The other answers they typed are shown back, but nothing is saved until the page is valid.

h4. Transit countries has its own title

The transit countries page's browser title reads "Transit countries - Import notification service - GOV.UK", and starts "Error: " when the page shows an error.

h4. Transit countries only applies to rail and road, once origin and commodity are answered

Opening the transit countries page when the saved means of transport is Air, Sea or not answered shows Overview instead. The same happens by rail or road while no country of origin or no commodity is saved, and Overview leaves the transit countries task out until both are saved. Overview also leaves out Identification details until a country of origin is saved, so it never links to a task the journey would turn away. If the means of transport changes to Air while the page is open, any submit from it (Add country, Remove, or either save button) shows Overview and saves no countries.

h4. Transit countries can be territories

The transit country search offers every country *and territory* the country of origin page offers, alphabetically. It is one fixed list from the reference data service: it does not change with the country of origin chosen. A territory reads with its country, for example "Canary Islands (Spain)", on the search with or without JavaScript, in the list of countries added, and on the review page.

h3. Acceptance criteria

Copied from the service's behaviour specification. All 14 scenarios this theme added are listed in full.

h4. A required means of transport

h5. Save and continue with no means of transport is refused

* *GIVEN* the user is on the arrival details page with nothing answered
* *WHEN* they save and continue
* *THEN* they stay on the arrival details page
* *AND* the error summary and the means of transport both show "Select a means of transport to the port of entry"
* *AND* the browser page title starts "Error: "

h5. A refused save shows the other answers back and saves nothing

* *GIVEN* the user has chosen a port of entry and entered an arrival date but no means of transport
* *WHEN* they save and continue
* *THEN* the port of entry and arrival date are shown back
* *AND* re-opening the page from the overview shows them empty

h5. Arrival details saved and returned without a means of transport is refused

* *GIVEN* the Arrival details page with a port of entry chosen and no means of transport
* *WHEN* the user selects "Save and return to overview"
* *THEN* they stay on the page showing "Select a means of transport to the port of entry" and the port of entry is still chosen
* *WHEN* they then open Overview
* *THEN* the Arrival details task reads "To do"
* *AND* opening the task shows the port of entry empty

h4. The transit countries page's title

h5. The browser title is Transit countries when the page loads

* *GIVEN* the user opens the transited countries page
* *WHEN* the page loads
* *THEN* the browser title is "Transit countries - Import notification service - GOV.UK"

h5. The browser title starts with Error when the page shows an error

* *GIVEN* the user is on the transited countries page
* *WHEN* they press "Add country" with nothing chosen
* *THEN* the browser title is "Error: Transit countries - Import notification service - GOV.UK"

h4. When transit countries applies

h5. Opening the page with air, sea or no means saved shows Overview

* *GIVEN* the saved means of transport is Air or Sea, or none is saved
* *WHEN* the user opens the transited countries page
* *THEN* Overview is shown

h5. Submitting the page after the means became air shows Overview and saves no countries

* *GIVEN* the transited countries page is open and the means of transport has since been saved as Air
* *WHEN* the user submits the page with Add country, Remove, Save and continue or Save and return to overview
* *THEN* Overview is shown
* *AND* no transited countries are saved

h5. Opening or submitting the page by rail or road without a country of origin or a commodity shows Overview and saves no countries

* *GIVEN* the saved means of transport is Rail or Road
* *AND* no country of origin is saved, or no commodity is saved
* *WHEN* the user opens the transited countries page, or submits it with Add country, Remove, Save and continue or Save and return to overview
* *THEN* Overview is shown
* *AND* no transited countries are saved

h4. What Overview shows before the earlier answers are saved

h5. Rail or road without a country of origin or a commodity has no Transit countries task

* *GIVEN* Rail or Road is saved as the means of transport
* *AND* no country of origin is saved, or no commodity is saved
* *WHEN* the user views Overview
* *THEN* no "Transit countries" task is shown
* *AND* "Arrival details" is still shown

h5. A commodity that needs identifiers with no country of origin has no identification row

* *GIVEN* the user has chosen a commodity that carries identifiers
* *AND* no country of origin is saved
* *WHEN* they view Overview
* *THEN* no Identification details row is shown
* *AND* "What are you importing?" is still shown

h4. Transit countries as territories

h5. The search offers the origin countries and their territories in alphabetical order

* *GIVEN* the user is on the transited countries page
* *WHEN* the search list is read in full
* *THEN* it holds exactly the countries and territories the country of origin page offers, by name, in alphabetical order

h5. Each territory is named with its country, with or without JavaScript

* *GIVEN* the transited countries search, with or without JavaScript
* *WHEN* a territory is read
* *THEN* it reads "<territory> (<country>)", for example "Canary Islands (Spain)"

h5. A territory is listed, saved and shown again under the same name

* *GIVEN* the user is on the transited countries page
* *WHEN* they add a territory and save and continue
* *THEN* no error is shown
* *AND* when they reopen the page the territory is listed as "<territory> (<country>)"

h5. A transited territory is named with its country

* *GIVEN* a road notification transiting the Canary Islands and France
* *WHEN* the user views the review
* *THEN* the transited countries row reads "Canary Islands (Spain), France"
