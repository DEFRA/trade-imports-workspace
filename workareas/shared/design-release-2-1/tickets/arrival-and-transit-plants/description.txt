h3. Background

This theme makes no change to the high-risk plants journey.

When the theme was first built, on 13 October, it reworded the plants arrival details page and made each place-of-landing option read "<port name> - <port code>". Before the theme was played again, design snagging (EUDPA-720, EUDPA-730) and reference lists delivered both to the plants journey in their reviewed form, and they are already live on main. So the theme's plants branch now carries nothing main does not have.

The rest of the theme is live animals only: a required means of transport and the transit countries page, which the plants journey does not ask. That work is on the live animals ticket, EUDPA-694.
