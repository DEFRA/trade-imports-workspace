h3. Placeholder

This is the Design Release 2.1 manage notifications theme: what a trader can do to a notification they have already submitted. Amend goes through a confirmation page before the amend review opens, cancelling an amendment is one question and two controls, copy as new carries over the trade answers and clears the shipment answers, and the delete confirmation page identifies the notification in a summary. It holds 6 rows across the live animals frontend, the high-risk plants frontend, the live animals backend, the test suite and the performance suite, 5 of them ready to build.

One row is dropped: inc-201 would have let a notification needing action be amended, copied and deleted as a submitted one can, and the 16 November ruling introduces no "Submitted action required" status. The pop-ups the prototype shows for these actions wait in the waiting-on-design theme; the confirmations stay pages, under claim 19 of the 9 October ruling.

This ticket exists to give the theme's branch a number. Its description and its acceptance criteria are filled in automatically from the behaviour specification once the build completes, and the theme is then split into one Story per service domain.
