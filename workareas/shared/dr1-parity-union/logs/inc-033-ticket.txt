h2. Increment

{{inc-033}} from the {{shared/dr1-parity-union}} backlog.

Design release 1 gives the consignment details their own hub task with their own status; the frontend folds them into the "What are you importing?" row.

On the frontend hub there is no task row for the consignment details. The words "Commodity details" do appear, but as the heading of the second section — "2. Commodity details" — over two rows about additional details and animal identification, not as a task of their own. The consignment-details page hangs off the "What are you importing?" row instead: that row covers the commodity search and the consignment-details page together ({{src/server/app/sets/live-animals/journeys/linear/flow/task-rows.js}} lines 29-33) and carries one status for both, so a user reading the hub cannot tell whether the numbers, packages and net weight have been entered — only whether the row as a whole is finished.

Design release 1 lists "Commodity details" as the first task under "2. Description of the goods", linking to {{/consignment-details}} and carrying its own "To do" or "Complete" tag independently of "What are you importing?" ({{app/routes.js}} lines 5770-5777).

h2. Acceptance criteria

* The hub shows a task row labelled "Commodity details" that links to {{/consignment-details}}.
* That row is the first task in the section Design release 1 calls "2. Description of the goods".
* The "Commodity details" row carries its own status tag, derived only from the consignment-details answers, independently of the "What are you importing?" row.
* The "What are you importing?" row covers the commodity selection only, and its status no longer depends on the consignment-details answers.
* The split of obligations between the two rows is settled and recorded — each obligation the {{commodityLines}} collection carries belongs to exactly one of the two rows.
* A new entry exists in {{src/server/app/sets/live-animals/journeys/linear/flow/task-rows.js}} for the consignment-details page and the parts of the commodity-lines collection that page collects.
* On a partly answered notification a user can see at a glance that the commodity selection and the commodity details are complete while the identification details are still to do.
* Frontend unit tests and the end-to-end specs cover the two rows and their independent statuses, and the visual baselines are updated.
