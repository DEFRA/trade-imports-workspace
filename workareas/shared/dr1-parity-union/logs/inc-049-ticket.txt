h2. Context

The CPH page is headed "County Parish Holding (CPH)" and the field beneath it carries the same string as its label. On the Consignment addresses page the same thing is called "County Parish Holding number (CPH)". So a trader meets three spellings of one term across two pages.

Design release 1 heads the page with the instruction "Add the county parish holding number (CPH)" and labels the field simply "CPH number". On the addresses page the row is headed "County parish holding (CPH) number". The term is lower case throughout, and "number" follows the bracketed abbreviation rather than preceding it.

* The check-your-answers row in the "Roles and addresses" card is labelled "County parish holding (CPH) number".
