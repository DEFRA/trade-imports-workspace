h2. Increment

{{inc-050}} from the {{shared/dr1-parity-union}} backlog.

DR1's CPH page explains what a CPH number is and where to find one, behind a "What is a CPH number?" expander. The frontend page carries no help content at all: it goes straight from the heading to the single input, and the feature's copy file holds only a title, a label, a hint and three error messages.

h2. Acceptance criteria

* A collapsed {{govukDetails}} expander, summarised "What is a CPH number?", sits between the page heading and the CPH number input on the frontend CPH number page.
* The expander holds two paragraphs. The first reads "A county parish holding (CPH) number is a unique 9-digit number used to identify land and buildings where livestock are kept, moved or handled."
* The second paragraph reads "You can find your CPH number on documents from the Animal and Plant Health Agency (APHA) or by checking your holding details on GOV.UK."
* DR1's own href for the GOV.UK holding-details clause is a placeholder, so either the real GOV.UK holding-details page is used or the second paragraph ships with no link at all, naming APHA documents only. Do not ship a placeholder href.
* All new strings live in {{src/server/app/sets/live-animals/journeys/linear/features/cph-number/copy/copy.en.js}}, not hard-coded in the template.
* The template change is made in {{src/server/app/sets/live-animals/journeys/linear/features/cph-number/template.njk}} and the existing form, hint and error-summary behaviour is unchanged.
* Frontend unit tests cover the expander's presence and copy, and the E2E specs and visual baselines covering the CPH number screen are updated and green.
