h2. Increment

{{inc-059}} from the {{shared/dr1-parity-union}} backlog.

Today the whole of the frontend's guidance on the permanent address question is one sentence, "A permanent address is required for this animal.", set under a small "Permanent address" heading in the middle of the identification card ({{src/server/app/sets/live-animals/journeys/linear/features/commodities/copy/copy.en.js}} lines 90-92). It says the address is needed. It does not say what a permanent address is, who will use it, or what happens if the trader invents one.

Design release 1 puts three blocks above the question: a GOV.UK warning text, a two-bullet definition naming the Animal and Plant Health Agency, and the question itself ({{app/views/permanent-address-animals.html}} lines 37-64).

h2. Acceptance criteria

* The identification page shows a {{govukWarningText}} component, with its exclamation icon, carrying the text "Providing a false address is an act of fraud".
* Below the warning the page shows the lead-in "A permanent address is where an animal:" followed by two bullets, "will permanently reside" and "can be checked by the Animal and Plant Health Agency (APHA)".
* The question is presented as the heading "Where will their permanent address be?".
* The single sentence "A permanent address is required for this animal." is replaced by those three blocks, not left alongside them.
* The copy change is made in {{src/server/app/sets/live-animals/journeys/linear/features/commodities/copy/copy.en.js}} and its Welsh sibling is updated to match.
* No new data and no new field are introduced.
* Falsifier cleared: the rendered identification page mentions fraud, permanent residence and the Animal and Plant Health Agency, where the captured DOM of {{fe-animal-identification}} previously contained none of the words "fraud", "reside" or "APHA".
* Existing unit tests and the E2E suite pass, with visual baselines updated where the new copy changes them.
