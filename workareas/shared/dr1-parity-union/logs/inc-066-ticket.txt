h2. Increment

{{inc-066}} from the {{shared/dr1-parity-union}} backlog.

The animal permanent address block ends with a mandatory Country select offering thirty-two countries under the placeholder "Select a country". Design release 1 asks for eight fields and no country, because the page has already said the address is somewhere the animal will permanently reside and can be checked by the Animal and Plant Health Agency (APHA) — an address APHA can inspect is a Great Britain address, so there is nothing to choose.

h2. Acceptance criteria

* The Country select is removed from the animal permanent address block in {{src/server/app/sets/live-animals/journeys/linear/features/commodities/animal-identification/address/fields.js}} (lines 66-70 and 147-153).
* The block asks for the eight fields Design release 1 asks for and no others: name or organisation name, address line 1, address line 2 (optional), town or city, county (optional), postcode or zip code, email address, phone number.
* The two country validation messages, "Select a country" and "Select a country from the list", are removed from the copy and validation for this block, including the mandatory-message map in {{src/server/app/sets/live-animals/journeys/linear/features/commodities/copy/copy.en.js}}.
* Nothing else in the journey reads the removed country value, and the journey still completes end to end.
* Unit tests and E2E specs covering the animal permanent address block no longer expect a country control, and pass.
