h2. Acceptance criteria

* In {{src/server/app/sets/live-animals/journeys/linear/features/import-purpose/copy/copy.en.js}}, the Transfer of ownership - Sale/gift hint reads Design release 1's wording: {{its}} not {{it's}}, no comma after {{aim}}, {{for example}} in place of {{e.g.}}, and a closing full stop — "Any movement of an animal that has as its aim the sale of or the transfer of ownership of the animal ... with no sale involved (for example a gift)."
* The Breeding hint ends with a full stop — "... or produce offspring."
* The Racing, competition, show or training hint ends with a full stop — "Animals to participate in competitive or training events."
* The Production hint ends with a full stop — "... any other animal product or by-product."
* The other seven hints — Rescue, Research, Approved premises or body, Companion animal not for resale or rehoming, Slaughter, Fattening and Restocking — are unchanged, and so are all eleven labels and their order.
* The {{traveling}} spelling in the Companion animal hint is shared by both sides and must NOT be tidied in this change.
* The Welsh copy file is corrected in the same pass if the same drift is present in it.
* No behaviour change: copy strings only.
