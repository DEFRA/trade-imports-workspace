h2. Increment

{{inc-076}} from the {{shared/dr1-parity-union}} backlog.

Check your answers never shows the exit answers — destination country, exit date and port of exit — so a trader cannot see or change them from the review page. Design release 1 lists each of them under the reason for import.

The frontend asks three further questions once a reason for import has been chosen — the destination country for a transit or transhipment consignment, and the exit date and port of exit for a temporary admission — each on a page of its own. None of the three ever reaches Check your answers. The card that holds the reason for import carries exactly four possible rows: Certified for, Includes unweaned animals, Reason for import and Purpose in the market ({{src/server/app/sets/live-animals/journeys/linear/features/check-answers/view-model/cards/consignment/additional-animal-details.js}} lines 18-54), and the check-answers copy file has no key for a destination country, an exit date or a port of exit at all.

Design release 1 adds a row per exit answer to its "Main reason for import" card, chosen by the reason: "Destination country" for a transhipment or a transit, and "Exit date" and "Port of exit" for a temporary admission of horses ({{app/routes.js}} lines 4640-4669).

h2. Acceptance criteria

* Check your answers shows a "Destination country" row under the reason for import when the reason is transit, transhipment or onward travel, carrying the answer the trader gave.
* Check your answers shows an "Exit date" row and a "Port of exit" row under the reason for import when the reason is temporary admission of horses, each carrying the answer the trader gave.
* Each of the three rows is hidden when its own question is out of scope, on the same condition the obligations already use in {{src/server/app/sets/live-animals/obligations/sections/import-reason.js}}.
* Each row has a Change link back to the page that collects it — {{fe-destination-country}}, {{fe-exit-date}} and {{fe-port-of-exit}} respectively.
* The three row labels are added to the check-answers copy file {{src/server/app/sets/live-animals/journeys/linear/features/check-answers/copy/copy.en.js}} rather than hard-coded in the view model.
* No "Exit border control post" row is added — the frontend collects no such answer, and that gap is out of scope for this increment.
* Unit tests cover each row appearing for its in-scope reason and being absent for an out-of-scope reason.
