h2. Increment

{{inc-097}} from the {{shared/dr1-parity-union}} backlog.

Frontend tells a trader the certification purpose is on "the health certificate". Design release 1 names the document, the ITAHC. A trader importing live animals is holding several certificates, so "the health certificate" does not say which one to look at, and the line uses a contraction where the rest of the service does not.

h2. Acceptance criteria

* The hint under the question "What are the animals certified for?" reads "This information can be found on the ITAHC."
* The change is made in {{src/server/app/sets/live-animals/journeys/linear/features/additional-details/copy/copy.en.js}}, with the Welsh copy sibling updated to match.
* The rendered additional details page contains the string {{ITAHC}}.
* Nothing else on the page changes. The sixteen certification purpose options keep the same labels in the same order, from "Further keeping" through to "Other", and the unweaned animals question and its hint are untouched.
* The page heading and the size of the two question labels are out of scope. They are raised separately as {{identification--page-headings-name-the-animal}} and {{identification--certification-questions-set-small}}.
* If the ITAHC abbreviation is expanded on first use elsewhere in the service, check this page is not the first place a trader meets it.
* Frontend unit tests pass, and the end-to-end specs pass with any visual baselines updated.
