h2. Increment

{{inc-100}} from the {{shared/dr1-parity-union}} backlog.

Frontend will not accept a notification until every animal has been identified. Design release 1 accepts one with no identifiers at all and asks for them after submission.

Today the animal-identifier group carries a count rule as well as a content rule: each unit record must hold at least one of the six identifiers, and the number of unit records on a commodity line must equal the number of animals declared for that line ({{recordCountEquals}} against {{numberOfAnimals}}, in {{src/server/app/sets/live-animals/obligations/sections/commodities/identifiers.js}} lines 98-101). That group is the whole of the {{animalIdentification}} task row, and readiness demands every task row be fulfilled, not applicable or optional. Readiness gates both the review section and the submit itself. So a trader declaring two hundred salmon must enter two hundred records before the service will show them their own notification.

Design release 1's own stated rule is "Documents remain optional. Animal identifiers are optional unless multiple species are selected, in which case at least one identifier is required per species" ({{app/routes.js}} lines 4562-4566).

h2. Acceptance criteria

* A single-species notification with no animal identifier records saved can reach the review section and be submitted.
* {{recordCountEquals}} against {{numberOfAnimals}} is dropped from the {{requires}} of the unit-record obligation in {{src/server/app/sets/live-animals/obligations/sections/commodities/identifiers.js}}, so an under-identified commodity line no longer holds the {{animalIdentification}} task row out of FULFILLED.
* The per-record rule is kept: a saved animal record must still carry at least one of the six identifiers.
* Identifiers are required for submission only where more than one commodity line carries a typed identifier set, and then only one complete record per line, never one per animal.
* The identifier count is still shown on the hub and on Check your answers as progress. It must not stop a submission.
* No backend or contract change is made. The animals backend's submit transition rejects on status only, never on content.
* The tests repo's end-to-end specs and visual baselines are updated for the new submit gate.
