Which of the four specific types appear is decided by four commodity allowlists in src/server/app/sets/live-animals/services/commodities/stub.js:107-115. The word microchip appears nowhere in src/server outside a test fixture, where it is typed as a value into the free-text field. The gap bites hardest on the commodities that need it: because dogs and horses do have specific identifiers, the free-text fallback is switched off for them, so a trader importing a dog is shown a passport and a tattoo field and nothing else. The captured DOM of fe-animal-identification bears this out — the dog line offers animalIdentifierPassport-2 and animalIdentifierTattoo-2, the horse line animalIdentifierPassport-1 and horseName-1, and there is nowhere on the page to put a chip number.\n\nDesign release 1 asks for a microchip on both identifier sets a companion animal or an equine falls into. Its identifiers are declared per commodity code, and a horse (0101) is asked for Microchip, Passport and Horse name while a cat, dog, ferret or other live mammal — all of them commodity code 01061900 — is asked for Microchip, Passport and Tattoo (app/data/commodity-identifiers.js:3-19). Microchip is the first field on both panels, so it is the identifier the design puts in front of the trader first. Microchipping is how a dog is identified in law, so the field is not decoration.\n\nAdd a microchip identifier and apply it to the equine and companion-animal commodities — horse, cat, dog and ferret, not the dog and horse alone. In Frontend that is a seventh obligation beside passport, tattoo, ear tag and horse name in src/server/app/sets/live-animals/obligations/sections/commodities/identifiers.js, a MICROCHIP_COMMODITIES allowlist beside the existing four in src/server/app/sets/live-animals/services/commodities/stub.js:107-115 written in the stub's picker-name vocabulary, a label and an error message in src/server/app/sets/live-animals/journeys/linear/features/commodities/copy/copy.en.js:58-72, the new obligation id added to the unit record's requires.anyOfIds so a microchip on its own satisfies the at-least-one-identifier rule, and its addition to the specific-identifier union so the free-text fallback keeps behaving as it does today.
