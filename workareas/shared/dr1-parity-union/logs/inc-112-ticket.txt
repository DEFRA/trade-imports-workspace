The additional details page asks two questions — "What are the animals certified for?" and "Does the consignment contain any unweaned animals?" — and sets both fieldset legends at the small size, {{govuk-fieldset__legend--s}}. At 19px they are barely larger than the hints and option labels beneath them, so the sixteen certification radios and the two unweaned radios run together as one list rather than reading as two separate questions.

Design release 1 sets the same two legends at the medium size, {{govuk-fieldset__legend--m}} with the shared heading class {{app-eu-heading-m}} ({{app/views/additional-animal-details.html:50}} and {{:72}}). The two questions stand out from their options, and the page reads as a heading followed by two questions rather than as one long form.
