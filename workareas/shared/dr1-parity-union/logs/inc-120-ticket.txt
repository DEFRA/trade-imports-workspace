h2. Increment

{{inc-120}} from the {{shared/dr1-parity-union}} backlog.

The hint under "Transport identification" is one run-on sentence: "To identify the means of transport, enter (one of the following): flight number; train number; road vehicle registration number; vessel name (for ferries, also the road vehicle registration number)". The four alternatives are separated only by semicolons, and the last one carries its own parenthesis, so a reader has to parse punctuation to see that there are four choices and where the fourth ends.

Design release 1 renders the same hint as a lead-in sentence, "To identify the means of transport, enter (one of the following):", followed by a bulleted list of the four items. Each alternative sits on its own line and the number of choices is visible without reading.

h2. Acceptance criteria

* The transport identification hint is a lead-in sentence, "To identify the means of transport, enter (one of the following):", followed by a bulleted list of four items.
* The four items read exactly as they do today: flight number, train number, road vehicle registration number, and vessel name (for ferries, also the road vehicle registration number) — in that order, wording unchanged.
* The copy entry in {{src/server/app/sets/live-animals/journeys/linear/features/transport/copy/copy.en.js}} becomes a lead sentence plus a list of items, rather than one string.
* {{src/server/app/sets/live-animals/journeys/linear/features/transport/port-of-entry/port-of-entry.njk}} renders the list as GDS bullet markup instead of passing a bare string as the hint text.
* The rendered DOM of the arrival details page contains a bulleted list inside the transport identification hint.
* Unit tests and the E2E suite pass, and any affected visual baselines are updated.
