h2. Increment

{{inc-123}} from the {{shared/dr1-parity-union}} backlog.

The frontend asks which type of transporter is moving the animals before showing any list, and the answer forks the journey into a commercial list or a blank private form that never meet. Design release 1 opens the task on one searchable list of every approved transporter, commercial and private together, and asks the type only when a user adds one that is not listed.

h2. Acceptance criteria

* The transporter section opens on one page listing every transporter available to the trader, commercial and private together, carrying the DAERA and APHA authorisation guidance and the introduction "This is the person or company responsible for transporting the consignment."
* {{/transporters}}, the route that asks the transporter type today, becomes that list page: one row per record showing its type, with a radio to pick one.
* The commercial-or-private question moves off the linear journey onto an add route reached from an "Add a transporter" button beneath the list, and is asked only of a trader who could not find their transporter.
* The two branches the type question forks into become the two ways of adding a transporter rather than the two ways of choosing one.
* The transporter fixture at {{src/server/app/services/commercial-transporters/records.js}} is widened to carry private transporters alongside commercial ones.
* The flow declaration at {{src/server/app/sets/live-animals/journeys/linear/flow/flow.js}} and the transport obligations at {{src/server/app/sets/live-animals/obligations/sections/transport.js}} declare the new order and gating, with unit tests updated to match.
* The E2E specs and visual baselines in the tests repo are updated for the new page order.
* Out of scope: the wording on the type chooser and on the private form, raised separately as {{transport--transporter-type-chooser-copy-differs}}; and the guidance link wording, raised as {{transport--transporter-guidance-link-differs}}.
