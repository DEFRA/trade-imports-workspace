h2. Increment

{{inc-129}} from the {{shared/dr1-parity-union}} backlog.

The calendar toggle at the right-hand end of the arrival date field on the port-of-entry page is a pale grey square with a dark grey calendar icon — the Ministry of Justice date picker component's own default. The frontend imports that component's stylesheet and adds no override ({{src/client/stylesheets/application.scss:2}}). Beside a field with a black border it reads as an inactive part of the input rather than as something to press.

Design release 1 paints the same toggle as a blue button — GOV.UK blue with a darker blue bottom edge and a white calendar icon — so it reads as the button it is, and darkens on hover ({{app/assets/sass/application.scss:33-67}}).

This is the same new stylesheet rule as {{inc-128}} (open calendar covers the questions below it), so the two should be done in one visit rather than twice.

h2. Acceptance criteria

* The date picker toggle button on the port-of-entry arrival date field has a GOV.UK blue background and a white calendar icon.
* The toggle carries the darker blue inset bottom edge a GOV.UK button carries, and no border.
* The toggle darkens to the darker blue on hover.
* The button keeps its existing visually-hidden "Choose date" label and the same icon shape — neither changes.
* The rule lives in the frontend's own stylesheet ({{src/client/stylesheets/application.scss}}); the Ministry of Justice component is not forked or patched.
