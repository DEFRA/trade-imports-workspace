h2. Acceptance criteria

* The guidance link points at {{https://www.gov.uk/guidance/animal-welfare-in-transport#transporting-animals-in-connection-with-an-economic-activity}} instead of {{https://www.gov.uk/guidance/transporting-animals-in-great-britain}}.
* The href carries no Google Analytics linker parameters. Design release 1's prototype copy carries them; they are an artefact of how the URL was pasted, not part of the requirement.
* The link text ends "(opens in a new tab)" rather than "(opens in new tab)", so it reads "Find out how to transport animals in connection with an economic activity (opens in a new tab)".
* The closing sentence reads "Documents issued in any EU member state are not valid for use in GB." — "member state" in sentence case.
* The lead sentence about DAERA and APHA, the three bullets, and "Documents issued by DAERA are valid for use in GB." are left exactly as they are.
* All three changes are made in the one guidance object at {{src/server/app/sets/live-animals/journeys/linear/features/transport/copy/copy.en.js}}, lines 62 to 77.
* Where the block sits on the page is out of scope. That is settled by the flow change raised beside this one, {{inc-123}}, which moves the guidance onto the transporter list. This increment is only about what the block says.
