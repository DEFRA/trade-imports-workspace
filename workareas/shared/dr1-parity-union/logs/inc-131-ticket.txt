h2. Increment

{{inc-131}} from the {{shared/dr1-parity-union}} backlog.

The private transporter details form runs nine fields in one unbroken block: name, two address lines, town or city, county, postal or zip code, country, telephone number, email address. Nothing marks where the address stops and the contact details start, and the telephone number comes before the email address.

Design release 1 splits the same nine questions in two. The address block ends at the country field, and a second-level heading, *Enter contact details*, introduces the last two: email address first, then phone number. Design release 1 does the same on its commercial transporter form, so the split is how it presents a transporter's contact details generally.

h2. Acceptance criteria

* A second-level heading reading *Enter contact details* sits between the country field and the contact fields on the private transporter details form.
* Under that heading, the email address field comes before the phone number field.
* The nine fields themselves, their names and their validation are unchanged.
* The heading uses the govuk-frontend heading classes already used elsewhere in the journey, with no new custom CSS.
* Unit tests and the end-to-end specs and visual baselines that cover the private transporter details page are updated to match.
* Falsified by finding a heading between the country field and the contact fields on {{fe-transporter-private}} in the captured rendered DOM.
