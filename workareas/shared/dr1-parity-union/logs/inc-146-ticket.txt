h2. Increment

{{inc-146}} from the {{shared/dr1-parity-union}} backlog.

h2. Context

Check your answers puts a Change link on every row of every card, so a finished notification offers twenty-four of them — twenty-two on rows, plus two in the heading of the Species card. An unfinished notification shows eighteen, all on rows. Design release 1 puts one Change link in each card's heading bar, level with the card title, and none on any row; both the finished and the unfinished review page carry exactly eleven, one per card.

Every row in a card already resolves to the same destination page, so moving the link to the card heading loses no route.

h2. Acceptance criteria

* Each card in the check-answers view model carries one card-level action, pointing at the page that collects that card's answers.
* No individual row on check your answers carries a change action — {{summary-row.js}} no longer calls {{changeAction}}.
* The Species card collapses from two heading links to one.
* The visually hidden text moves with the link, naming the card rather than the row.
* Every card-level Change link keeps the {{change=1}} flag, so the trader is returned to the review afterwards.
* The finished and the unfinished check-answers page each show one Change link per card, and none on a row.

h2. Falsified by

Finding a Change link on a row of any card in the captured {{dr1-review-notification}}, or a card whose rows carry no Change link in the captured {{fe-check-answers}}.
