h2. Acceptance criteria

* The declaration page adds the sentence "I am complying with the requirements of Regulation (EU) 2017/625 including on animal health and welfare." beneath the first heading, "I am the contact for the authorities and located in the UK."
* The second heading is shortened to "I confirm I am responsible for this consignment." and is followed by "I am responsible from the submission of this notification to when it enters Great Britain. And I am responsible until it has either:" with two bullets, "cleared official checks at the border" and "reached the Place of Destination as stated on the health certificate or notification".
* The four accountability items read "any payment for the official controls at the border", "any arrangements to re-dispatch the consignment", "any costs needed for quarantine or isolation of consignments", and "any costs needed for destruction and disposal of consignments, when instructed by the authorities".
* The copy changes are made in {{src/server/app/sets/live-animals/journeys/linear/features/declaration/copy/copy.en.js}} and its Welsh counterpart, so both languages carry the Design release 1 wording.
* The declaration template renders a paragraph under the first heading and a paragraph plus a nested list under the second, so the new copy displays as Design release 1 sets it out.
* The two closing sentences and the checkbox label are left unchanged — they already match Design release 1 word for word.
* The E2E specs and visual baselines for the declaration page are updated to match the new copy.
