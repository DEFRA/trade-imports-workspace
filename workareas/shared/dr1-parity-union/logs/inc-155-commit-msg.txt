fix(parity-dr1): The read-only view of an already-submitted notification still ends with "Now submit your notification" and a Continue button; Design release 1 renders no form once a notification is submitted

The review page is already redrawn read-only once a notification is
submitted: the status tag turns green, every Change link is dropped, and
"Copy as new" and "Delete" appear at the top. The block at the foot of the
page was left outside that condition, so the page still carried the heading
"Now submit your notification", the sentence below it and a live Continue
button — offering the one action the read-only page had otherwise taken
away. Pressing it reached the declaration, which redirects a submitted
journey to the confirmation page, landing the trader on a confirmation for
a notification they did not just submit.

Wrap the heading, sentence and submitting form in `{% if not readOnly %}`,
so the read-only review ends with the last card and presents a submitted
notification as a record with no action on it. This matches Design release
1, which guards the whole form with the same read-only test.

Adds a fit spec covering the submitted review: it asserts the page is the
read-only one, that the submit heading, body and Continue button are all
absent, and that the submitted review has no serious or critical axe
violations.
