h2. Increment

{{inc-155}} from the {{shared/dr1-parity-union}} backlog.

The read-only view of an already-submitted notification still ends with the heading "Now submit your notification", the sentence "Continue to the declaration to submit your notification." and a green Continue button. The page has already told the trader the notification is submitted and has removed every Change link, yet it still invites them to submit it again. Pressing Continue goes to the declaration, which redirects a submitted journey straight to the confirmation page, so the trader lands on a confirmation for a notification they did not just submit.

Design release 1 wraps the whole form in a read-only test and renders nothing at the foot of the page for a submitted notification, so its read-only review ends with the last card.

h2. Acceptance criteria

* When the review page is rendered read-only for a submitted notification, the heading "Now submit your notification", the sentence "Continue to the declaration to submit your notification." and the Continue button are not rendered.
* The read-only review ends with the last summary card, with no form at the foot of the page.
* A draft review that is not read-only still shows the heading, the sentence and the Continue button exactly as it does today.
* The change is one condition on the existing {{readOnly}} flag around the closing block in {{src/server/app/sets/live-animals/journeys/linear/features/check-answers/template.njk}} lines 108 to 114, matching how that template already guards the Change links and the Copy and Delete buttons.
* E2E specs and visual baselines in the tests repo are updated to match the read-only review page.
