## 0. Decisions

1. **The session keeps no run record at all, so the cookie and the port slots go, not only `run-state.js`.** Criterion 1 says "no per-notification journey progress in the session or a cookie". Deleting `run-state.js` but keeping `session.openingRun`/`setOpeningRun` and the `openingRun` cookie would leave a mechanism nothing reads. So the cookie name leaves `DEFAULT_COOKIE_NAMES`, both sets' `SESSION_COOKIE_NAMES`, the second-set fixtures, and the port's slots, accessors and both adapters. `journeyCookieNames()` (animals) and the plants explicit lists then register and check one cookie fewer. Rejected: keeping the cookie registered "for old sessions". An unregistered cookie a browser still sends is parsed by Hapi as an unknown cookie and ignored; the yar key is never read. No migration is reachable.

2. **Plants' two hand-written cookie lists stay hand-written; they only lose the run cookie.** `P:app/engine/journey.js` `registerJourneyCookie` and `P:app/set-completeness.js` `cookieNamesOf` each list three names, where animals reads `journeyCookieNames()`. Converging plants on animals' `journeyCookieNames()` is a cross-twin structural clean-up, not this row's behaviour; it goes to follow-ups. The touched lines and functions follow the house rules: `P:app/set-completeness.js:8-22` and `:32-40` are rationale and caller-describing doc comments on module and internal helpers, deleted (their facts are already in `P:app/docs/add-a-set.md`'s mount-check passage; the implementor checks and adds there anything missing). `cookieNamesOf` and `assertJourneyCookiesRegistered` are internal helpers and keep no doc comment.
