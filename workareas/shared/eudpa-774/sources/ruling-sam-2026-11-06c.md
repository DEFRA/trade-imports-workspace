# Ruling: Sam, 6 November 2026 (third)

Sam's answer to the one question left by the second DISTIL run. The option is quoted with the question it answered.

> Question: "Back on the review page follows the trail. Where should it go when the trader reached the review page some other way: back from a page its Change link opened, after cancelling an amendment, from the declaration or confirmation page, after a refused Continue, by typing the address, or in a new session?"
> Answer (option chosen): "DISTIL's default (Recommended)": "Back from a page a Change link or error link opened: wherever Back went before, carried on that link. After a cancelled amendment: the dashboard. After a refused Continue that shows the review again (plants): wherever Back went before. Anything else, including from the declaration or confirmation, a typed address or a new session: Overview on a draft or amendment, the dashboard once submitted, as today. The declaration and confirmation pages are unchanged."

1. When the trader returns to the review page by Back from a page that one of its Change links or error-summary links opened, Back on the review page goes where it went before, carried on that link. Answers the question "Where Back on the review page goes when reached some other way" (c-053).
2. After a cancelled amendment, Back on the review page goes to the dashboard. Answers c-053.
3. In high-risk plants, after a refused Continue that shows the review page again itself, Back on the review page goes where it went before, carried on the form. Answers c-053.
4. Every other way of reaching the review page (the declaration's Back link, a refusal that comes back through the declaration in live animals, the confirmation page's View your notification link, a typed address, a new session) keeps today's Back: Overview on a draft or an amendment, the dashboard on a submitted notification. Answers c-053.
5. The declaration and the confirmation page are not changed. Answers c-053.
6. Back on the review page is built in the every-page row of the three-row backlog (claim 5 of ruling:sam-2026-11-06b), not in a new row. New.
