## What it settles

Ticket mode raises its pull requests and stops when every check is green. It posts the links and the evidence and
waits for Sam's go. It never merges its own pull requests.

Workspace rule 3 stands as written. Ticket mode needs no exemption beside the build loop's and the nightly jobs'.
