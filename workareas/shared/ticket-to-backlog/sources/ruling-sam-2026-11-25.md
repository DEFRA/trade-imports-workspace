# Ruling, 25 November 2026: combine rows that suit combining

Sam, on the ticket-to-backlog plan (said by voice; "roles" in the transcript means rows):

> Make sure that wherever the stage is, it takes the rows created and then looks at them and goes, "Oh, these seven
> rows could actually be three rows," and combines things that suit combining. There has been an attempt to split
> things up too much, and there's an overhead to each row, so we want to minimise needless creation.

## What it rules

- Every stage that creates rows (full DISTIL and ticket intake) ends with a combine review over the rows it made.
- Combining is the default. A row stands alone only with a reason written in its notes.
- Each row carries a fixed cost (ticket, branch, plan, reviews, ladder, PR, CI), so fewer, fuller rows win.
- Applies to this programme's own backlog too: its 7 drafted rows become 3.
